01 — Raw materials

Every company has its own culture, habits, strengths and story. When I join a new environment, my first move is always the same: understand before acting.

Discover the teams, listen to their needs, meet the partners, observe the products and the audiences they speak to.

This immersion phase is essential. It's what lets me take ownership of projects quickly afterwards, spot opportunities and bring tailored solutions rather than off-the-shelf answers.

This approach is what allowed me to build events and communications departments from scratch, as well as support already established structures in their growth.

Building on what exists, bringing a fresh perspective and quickly becoming a reliable point of contact for teams: that's how I like to work.

02 — Assembly

Events

A successful event starts long before the doors open. It takes the right format, a realistic schedule, coordinated suppliers, tracked budgets, anticipated hiccups, and an overview kept right down to the last detail.

Over the years, I've organised professional events, partner meetups, institutional milestones, open days and gatherings designed to bring people together. Very different projects, but with one thing in common: creating moments that bring people together and leave them wanting more of the brand experience.

Communication

Effective communication isn't just about posting regularly. It's about understanding what's worth telling, finding the right angle, adapting the message to the right audience, and creating consistency across every channel.

Social media, newsletters, websites, press relations, internal communication or editorial content: each tool speaks its own language, but they all need to tell the same story.

Partners, ambassadors & communities

Behind every project, there are people first. Building and maintaining lasting relationships with partners, supporting collaborations, coordinating public statements or representing a structure at events are all part of my everyday work.

I've learned that a network isn't built with contacts alone, but with trust, availability and genuine attention to human relationships.

A role that alternates between content creation, coordination, fieldwork, trade shows, competitions and product launches is exactly what I love about communication.

English

English isn't just a professional skill, it's a language I've always loved practising. I took a European section at middle and high school, chose an English specialism in my final year, earned a Cambridge certification during my studies, and scored 101/120 on the TOEFL.

In my professional experience, I've also had the chance to work with international contacts on a daily basis. English is still part of my everyday life today: I regularly read novels in their original language and watch most of my films in English.

Joining a Breton company with an international reach would be a great way to keep both things I particularly love alive: a strong local footing and a language that's been with me all along.

I have a strong appetite for projects that make sense, environments where ideas flow freely, and teams that like to build together. I believe deeply in the value of encounters: they're often what makes you want to fully commit to a project.
