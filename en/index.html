01 — Raw materials

Every company has its own language. A culture, habits, know-how, and people who bring a project to life every day.

When I join a new environment, I always start with the same thing: understand before acting. Meeting the teams, listening to the people who know the ground, talking with partners, discovering the products, understanding the audiences they speak to... I like taking the time to soak up a world before trying to tell its story.

This immersion phase is, to me, essential. It's what then allows me to move efficiently, spot the right opportunities and offer solutions built for the context, rather than off-the-shelf answers.

This approach is what allowed me to build events and communications departments from scratch, as well as support already established organisations in their growth.

Building on what exists, bringing a fresh perspective and quickly becoming a reliable point of contact for teams: that's how I like to practise this job.

02 — Assembly

Events

A successful event starts well before its doors open.

It's born upstream: imagining the right format, building a realistic schedule, coordinating teams, and staying alert, right up to the last moment, to head off any hiccup.

Over the years, I've imagined and run professional events, partner meetups, and gatherings designed to bring people together.

Very different projects, but always guided by the same drive: creating moments that bring people together, bring a brand to life, and leave participants with the memory of a great experience.

Communication

Relevant communication starts with a solid understanding of the ground.

Before telling a story, I like to listen to the teams, observe how things are actually used, and find the thread that will give meaning to the whole. Only then come the words and the materials.

Social media, newsletters, websites, press relations, internal communication or editorial content: each channel speaks its own language, but they all need to tell the same story, consistently. It's this search for balance between strategy, listening and storytelling that guides the way I communicate.

Partner relations

Behind every project, there are human relationships first.

Building and nurturing a network of partners, supporting collaborations, or coordinating public statements are all part of my everyday work.

Through my experience, I've learned that a lasting partnership doesn't rest on shared interests alone, but on the quality of exchanges, availability, listening, and the ability to build together. It's this attention to people that, in my view, makes it possible to create solid collaborations and make people want to commit to a shared project.

03 — Field applications

Fédération du BTP du Finistère

Structuring events and communication for a professional federation

  • When I arrived, everything was still to be built.
    I created the communications and events department, designing a strategy able to support both the teams and the members. I designed institutional events from A to Z, developed communication, ran partner and press relations, and helped raise the organisation's profile among local economic stakeholders.
    A deeply cross-functional role, where strategy, fieldwork and coordination always move forward together.

Result: a fully structured communications and events function, events that became genuine highlights for the network, and digital engagement more than doubled in six months

Délices du Kae

Bringing a brand to life,
on the ground and online

  • Across a network of four locations, I helped build a shared communications approach while designing events and activities able to bring the brand to life for customers, partners and teams.
    Between editorial strategy, brand identity, creating standout moments and operational coordination, I managed to make digital channels and on-the-ground experiences work together to tell a consistent story, whatever the touchpoint.

Result: events that became genuine local visibility drivers, a harmonised identity across all locations, and communities with lasting stronger engagement

Independent activity

Growing a brand from A to Z

  • For six years, I ran my own entrepreneurial project. A venture that led me to shape a brand identity, organise fun and educational events, build visibility entirely organically, and support over 250 clients every year.
    This experience taught me to steer every stage of a project, from strategic thinking to client relationships, with a full overview.
    A real school of rigour and initiative, whose lessons I still apply today.

Result: a lasting business, visibility built without any advertising, and a loyal, engaged community

No Boxes,

Result: a formative first experience, which taught me that communication is all the more useful when it makes teams' work easier and gives meaning to the projects they carry

05 — Manufacturing details

Mobility

Events have always been the moments where my job takes on another dimension.

They're a chance to meet teams, partners, clients and communities, and to see a brand come to life beyond a screen.

It's often on the ground that the most spontaneous conversations, the sharpest ideas and the clearest understanding of a project happen.

I like alternating between time for reflection, content creation, and days spent with the people who bring a company to life.

English

English isn't just a professional skill, it's a language I've always loved practising.

I took a European section at middle and high school, chose the English specialism in my final year, earned a Cambridge certification during my studies, and scored 101/120 on the TOEFL.

Throughout my career, it has naturally found its place in my exchanges with international contacts and in settings where it was part of everyday life. Today, it still accompanies my reading, my films and my series.

A person before a profile

I have a strong appetite for projects that make sense, environments where ideas flow freely, and teams that like to build together.

I believe deeply in the value of encounters: they're often what makes you want to fully commit to a project.
