01 — Raw materials

Every company has its own culture, habits, strengths and history. When I join a new environment, my first step is always the same: understand before acting.

Meet the teams, listen to their needs, get to know the partners, and become familiar with the products and the audiences they serve.

This immersion phase is essential. It's what then allows me to take ownership of projects quickly, spot opportunities and bring tailored solutions rather than off-the-shelf answers.

This approach is what allowed me to build events and communications departments from scratch, as well as support already established organisations in their growth.

Building on what exists, bringing a fresh perspective and quickly becoming a reliable point of contact for teams: that's how I like to work.

02 — Assembly

Events

A successful event starts long before its doors open.

It takes the right format, a realistic schedule, coordinated suppliers, tracked budgets, anticipated hiccups, and an overview kept right down to the last detail.

Over the years, I've organised professional events, partner meetups, institutional milestones, open days and gatherings designed to bring people together. Very different projects, but with one thing in common: creating moments that bring people together and leave participants with a real brand experience.

Communication

Useful communication starts with a solid understanding of the ground.

It's not just about posting regularly, but about knowing what's worth telling, finding the right angle, adapting the message to the right audience, and creating consistency across every channel.

Social media, newsletters, websites, press relations, internal communication or editorial content: each tool speaks its own language, but they all need to tell the same story.

Partner relations

Behind every project, there's a human relationship first.

Building and maintaining lasting relationships with partners, supporting collaborations, coordinating public statements or representing an organisation at events are all part of my everyday work.

I've learned that a network isn't built with contacts alone, but with trust, availability and genuine attention to human relationships.

05 — Manufacturing details

Mobility

Events have always been the moments where my job takes on another dimension.

They're a chance to meet teams, partners, clients and communities, and to see a brand come to life beyond a screen. A role that alternates between content creation, coordination, fieldwork, trade shows, competitions and product launches is exactly what I love most about this job.

English

English isn't just a professional skill, it's a language I've always loved practising.

I took a European section at middle and high school, chose an English specialism in my final year, earned a Cambridge certification during my studies, and scored 101/120 on the TOEFL.

It felt natural, then, to keep a place for English throughout my career, working regularly with international contacts and operating in settings where English was part and parcel of everyday exchanges. Today, it still accompanies my reading as much as the films and series I watch.

Joining a Breton company with an international reach would be a great way to keep both things I particularly love alive: a strong local footing and a language that's been with me all along.

A person before a profile

My everyday life today is deeply tied to Finistère: its landscapes, its coastline, its natural spaces and the people who bring their projects to life there. I love gardens, plants, long walks along the coast, and bookshops where you stay longer than planned.

I have a strong appetite for projects that make sense, environments where ideas flow freely, and teams that like to build together. I believe deeply in the value of encounters: they're often what makes you want to fully commit to a project.
